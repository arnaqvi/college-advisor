## Our Story

[App Name] started with a simple observation: the best college guidance shouldn't depend on your zip code or your budget. Too many talented students miss out on scholarships, dream schools, or better-fit programs simply because no one showed them the roadmap.

We set out to build a college advisor that's smart enough to give personalized guidance, affordable enough for any family, and simple enough to actually use — from freshman year through the final decision letter.

---

## Our Mission

To make expert-level college guidance accessible to every student, regardless of their family's income or background — through smart, affordable, and personalized advising tools.

- **Deadline & Task Tracking** — Never miss an application, financial aid, or scholarship deadline again.
- **Essay & Application Support** — Tools and feedback to help students put their best work forward.

## Why Families Choose Us

**Affordable for real families.** Our plans start free, and our paid tiers cost a fraction of traditional counseling — because we believe great advice shouldn't be a luxury.

**Built for the whole family.** Our Family Plan supports up to three students under one account, because we know college prep often happens for more than one child at a time.

**No pressure, no gatekeeping.** We give students and parents the tools to make informed decisions — not a sales pitch for any particular school.
